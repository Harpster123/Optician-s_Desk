Oblique Cylinder Power

Meridian power at any axis: F(θ) = S + C·sin²(θ − α) (θ, α in degrees). Gives power at a target meridian (useful for toric optics reasoning).

Tip: At θ = α (the cyl axis), sin²(0)=0 → F(θ)=S. At θ = α+90°, sin²(90°)=1 → F(θ)=S+C (the power in the strong meridian).
