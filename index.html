Quick Reference

Standard Fitting Measurements

Dispensing norms & tolerances

Pantoscopic tilt8–12°
Back vertex distance12–14 mm
Face form / wrap angle0–5°
Monocular PD tolerance± 1.0 mm

Measure with frame fully adjusted and in its final position on the patient's face.

Progressive Lens Engravings

Reading the permanent markings

Fitting heights are normally anywhere from 0–6 mm above the reference point. Add power and lens etching both sit below the reference marks. Use a lensometer or pen marker to re-locate if temporary markings are removed.

Refractive Index Guide

Material reference by index

1.50 — CR-39Standard
1.53 — TrivexImpact resistant
1.60 — MR-8Mid-index
1.67 — MR-10High-index
1.74 — MR-174Ultra-thin

Higher index = thinner & lighter but lower Abbe value. Trivex has the highest Abbe (45) of any mid/high-index material.
